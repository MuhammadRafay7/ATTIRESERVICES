TECHNICAL PLATE (drawn in code, no licence obligation)
-------------------------------------------------------
One portfolio entry still has no photograph and renders as a dimensioned
schematic — src/components/ProductPlate.tsx:

  P-11 Finished leather and hides   plate "hide"

To swap it for a photograph, add `image: "/photos/<file>.jpg"` to that entry
in src/lib/content.tsx — the `plate` field is then ignored.
